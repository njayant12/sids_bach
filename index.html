🌲 Why Portland?

The Perfect Destination

Portland offers the ultimate bachelor party experience with incredible hiking trails, an amazing vegetarian food scene, craft breweries, and of course - a Rockets game! From Powell's Books to Forest Park, from food carts to fine dining, Portland has it all.

🎯 Activities & Experiences

🥾 Hiking Adventures

Forest Park: One of the largest urban forests in the US with over 80 miles of trails. Perfect for morning hikes with stunning city views.

Columbia River Gorge: World-class waterfalls including Multnomah Falls (just 30 min away).

🍔 Vegetarian Food Scene

Virtuous Pie: Incredible vegan pizza and ice cream

Harlow: Farm-to-table vegetarian comfort food

Departure: Upscale Asian fusion with great veggie options and rooftop views

🍺 Craft Beer & Breweries

Portland has 75+ breweries! Hit up Deschutes Brewery, Breakside Brewery, or do a brewery tour in the Pearl District.

🌃 Nightlife

Explore the East Burnside area, check out Ground Kontrol (classic arcade bar), or hit up the vibrant Mississippi Avenue district.

☕ Coffee Culture

Start your mornings at Stumptown Coffee or Coava Coffee Roasters - Portland takes coffee seriously!

🎲 Unique Experiences

Powell's City of Books (largest independent bookstore), Salt & Straw ice cream, Portland Japanese Garden, or catch a comedy show at Helium Comedy Club.

💡 Pro Tips

🏠 Where to Stay

Book an Airbnb in the Pearl District or Northwest Portland for easy access to everything. Downtown hotels put you near Moda Center.

🚗 Getting Around

Portland has great public transit (MAX Light Rail), but rent a car for the Columbia Gorge hike. Uber/Lyft readily available.

🌦️ Weather

January in Portland = rain likely! Pack layers, waterproof jackets, and good hiking boots. Temps around 40-50°F.
